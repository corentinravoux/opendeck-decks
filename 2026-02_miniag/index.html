ZTF calibration effort

  • ZTF = Zwicky Transient Facility
  • High-cadence photometric telescop in the Palomar observatory
  • Very large field of view (47 deg²)
  • Transient sky: Supernovae, gamma ray burst, tidal distruptive events, comets, asteroids

ZTF calibration effort

  • IN2P3 effort on calibrating ZTF supernovae luminosity
  • 4 labs involved: LPCA, IP2I (Lyon), CPPM (Marseille) & LPNHE (Paris)
  • Effort close to completion (ZTF DR2.5): organization of small workshop to focus on finalizing data and paper completion
  • Decision and application of a blinding scheme
  • Room 106 (EUPI) in the campus: adapted for workshop format
