Some astronomy definitions

  • Transparency/Opacity: transmission of the atmosphere on the line of sight.
  • Sky level: Sky brightness from natural and artificial light sources (mag.arcsec⁻²)
  • Airmass: Amount of air in the line-of-sight (normalized to zenith).
  • Seeing: Blurring caused by atmosphere, quantified by FWHM of a star image (in arcsec)
  • Often normalized to a nominal atmosphere on the considered observation site

  • 1750/1755: Thomas Wright (English astronomer) & Emmanuel Kant postulate that nebulae are other galaxies like the Milky Way
    • "Une nébuleuse n’est pas un unique et énorme soleil, mais un système de nombreux soleils, rassemblés en raison de leur distance dans un espace si étroit, que leur lumière, qui serait imperceptible pour chacun d’eux isolément, parvient, grâce à leur innombrable quantité, à produire une blancheur pâle et uniforme.” – Emmanuel Kant, Histoire générale de la nature et théorie du ciel, 1755
  • 1910-20's:
    • Leavitt & Hubble observations confirmed the existence of other galaxies
    • Hubble & Lemaitre discovers expansion

LSST survey

  • 10-year photometric survey of half of the sky (~ 20 000 deg²)
  • Started in june 2026
  • Complete coverage every 4 nights during 10 years (static and transient survey)
  • 15 TB data per night, 100 PB in total, 10 million alerts per night in average
  • Deliverables:
    • 20 billion galaxies with photometric redshifts up to z=3
    • 17 billion resolved stars
    • ~ 1 million type Ia supernovae (with ~ 300,000 cosmological SNe Ia)
