Hydro simulations

  • Nyx simulation code:
    • Dark matter particles with N-body and Particle Mesh
    • Baryons resolved with full hydrodynamic simulations
  • GPU version: highly vectorizable
  • Initialization with Zel'dovic approximation at z=200
  • Computation of P3D/P1D with gimlet (MPI code)

The ACCEL² simulation suite

  • Simulation grid: [160 - 640] Mpc/h, Resolution range: [25 - 100] kpc/h
  • Proper modeling of Lyman-α forest needs a resolution four times higher than the Jeans scale (~25 kpc/h)
  • Time-varying, but spatially uniform, ultraviolet background
  • Rescaling of mean transmitted flux to match observations
  • One thermal history for now (can be done in post-processing)

Lukic et al. 2015, Chabanier et al. 2023
